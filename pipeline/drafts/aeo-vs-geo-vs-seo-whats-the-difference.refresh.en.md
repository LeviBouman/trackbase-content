SEO, AEO and GEO are three layers of one goal, getting your brand found, aimed at three different kinds of system. SEO targets search engines and ranks pages, AEO targets answer engines and gets your content quoted as the direct answer, and GEO targets generative AI models such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini and gets your brand named inside a synthesized response. They overlap, but they are measured differently, and treating them as one is how brands end up invisible in the channels that matter most.

## The three acronyms side by side

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full name | Search Engine Optimization | Answer Engine Optimization | Generative Engine Optimization |
| Target | Google, Bing results pages | Featured snippets, AI Overviews, boxed answers | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok and other LLMs |
| You win when | Your page ranks and gets the click | Your content is lifted into the answer | Your brand is mentioned, cited or recommended |
| Main metric | Rankings, clicks, traffic | Answer and snippet presence | Mentions, citations, sentiment, Share of Voice |
| Click required | Yes | Often no | Often no |

## What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the discipline of ranking pages on traditional search engines like Google and Bing. Success is measured in clicks: rank higher, get more traffic. The playbook is well known: backlinks, page speed, keyword targeting and technical health.

SEO still works, and it remains the foundation for the other two. But fewer searches end in a click. Many users now get their answer directly from the results page, or skip the search engine entirely and ask an assistant. That does not make SEO obsolete; it means ranking is no longer the only place your brand can win or lose.

## What is AEO?

[Answer Engine Optimization](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/answer-engine-optimization) (AEO) is about being the source that gets quoted when an engine answers a question directly. Think Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets, or the boxed answers in Bing. AEO rewards content that:

- Answers a specific question in the first two or three sentences ([answer-first content](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/answer-first-content))
- Uses clear headings phrased as questions
- Includes [structured data](https://trackbase.ai/features/structured-data) such as FAQ schema
- Reads as authoritative and factual, not promotional

If SEO is about ranking a page, AEO is about being the answer. It is the bridge between classic search and generative AI, because the same formatting habits that win snippets also make content easier for language models to extract.

## What is GEO?

[Generative Engine Optimization](https://trackbase.ai/solutions/geo-optimization) (GEO) is the newest of the three. It targets large language models like [ChatGPT](https://trackbase.ai/blog/track-what-chatgpt-says-about-your-company), Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok and others that synthesize answers from many sources at once. There is often no link, no click and no obvious feedback loop.

GEO success looks different. Your brand needs to be mentioned, cited or recommended inside an AI-generated answer. That depends on three things:

1. **What the model knows.** Whether your content and brand were part of its training data or are reachable through live retrieval.
2. **How often you appear in context.** How consistently the web connects your brand with the topic, including third-party reviews, forums and publications.
3. **How you are described.** Whether the language used about you is positive, accurate and specific. See [why sentiment decides who wins](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins).

Answers also differ per model, so a single check proves little. We explain the mechanics in [how AI models decide which brands to recommend](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-ai-models-decide-which-brands-to-recommend).

## How they fit together

These disciplines layer rather than compete:

- SEO gets your pages crawled, indexed and ranked.
- AEO gets your content lifted into direct answers.
- GEO gets your brand into AI-synthesized responses.

Strong content and real authority help all three, so you do not need three separate content strategies. You need one strategy that is structured for extraction, supported by third-party sources and measured on all three layers.

## What to do first

1. **Keep your SEO foundation healthy.** Crawlable pages, fast load times and clean technical setup are prerequisites for everything else. Check that AI crawlers are not blocked by accident.
2. **Rewrite key pages answer-first.** Lead with the answer in two or three sentences, use question-style H2s, add a list or table and close with an FAQ.
3. **Add structured data.** FAQ, Organization and Article schema make your pages easier to parse for answer engines and AI systems.
4. **Earn mentions off-site.** Models lean on third-party sources, so reviews, comparison pages and trade press matter. See [how to get cited by AI](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-earning-mentions-on-the-sources-models-trust).

## FAQ

### Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. SEO remains the foundation because AI answers draw on pages that are crawlable, indexed and trusted. GEO adds a second objective on top: being named and cited inside generated answers. Most teams need both.

### What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO optimizes content to be quoted as the direct answer in features such as featured snippets and AI Overviews. GEO targets the wider set of generative models and aims for your brand to be mentioned, cited or recommended in synthesized answers, often without a click.

### How do I measure GEO?

Run a fixed set of prompts that mirror how your customers ask, repeat them daily across several AI models, and record mentions, citations, sentiment and Share of Voice against competitors. A tool automates this, because individual answers vary from run to run.
