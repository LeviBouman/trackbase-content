# How to Get Cited by AI: Earning Mentions on the Sources Models Trust

> Changing how AI talks about your brand starts outside your own website: independent citations weigh far more than your own marketing. Here is how to earn them.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-07-05 · Updated 2026-07-05 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-earning-mentions-on-the-sources-models-trust*

The fastest way to change how AI talks about your brand usually is not on your own website. It is everywhere else. A [citation, where the model uses you as a source](https://trackbase.ai/blog/mentions-vs-citations-why-both-matter-for-your-ai-visibility), carries more weight than any line you write about yourself, because [AI models weight independent mentions](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-ai-models-decide-which-brands-to-recommend) far above your own marketing copy.

**Owned content is what you control. Earned citations are what models trust. You need both.**

## Why earned beats owned here

A homepage tagline is a claim. A balanced review on a respected platform is evidence. Models behave the same way a skeptical buyer would: they discount what a brand says about itself and lean on what independent sources say instead. That is why the off-site map below moves the needle more than another landing page.

## The sources worth earning

Not every citation is equal, and models appear to discount obviously promotional content. Aim for credible, independent surfaces:

-   **Review platforms**: G2, Capterra and TrustRadius. Real, recent reviews from real customers carry strong signal, especially in software categories.

-   **Editorial and press**: coverage in respected industry publications beats anything self-published.

-   **Communities**: Reddit, Hacker News and niche forums where buyers compare notes. Models read these heavily.

-   **Wikipedia and Wikidata**, if your brand genuinely meets the notability bar. Do not fake it; the criteria are strict and gaming them backfires.

-   **Third-party comparison pages and listicles**: the “best of” and “X alternatives” content maintained by others.

## How to actually earn them

Treat this as a targeted campaign, not scattered outreach:

-   Find what AI already cites for your category. The sources feeding today’s answers are your priority list.

-   Rank those sources by signal, then go after the highest-value ones first.

-   Earn the placements: run customer review campaigns, pursue digital PR, contribute genuine expertise, and get onto credible comparison lists accurately.

-   Stay honest. A glowing affiliate post does less than a balanced write-up from a source readers and models actually trust.
