# How to Get Cited by AI: Earn Mentions on Trusted Sources

> Independent citations weigh far more than your own marketing. Here is how to find the sources AI already cites in your category and earn a place on them.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-07-05 · Updated 2026-11-04 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-earning-mentions-on-the-sources-models-trust*

The fastest way to change how AI talks about your brand usually is not on your own website. It is on the sources models already trust. A [citation, where the model uses a page as its source](https://trackbase.ai/blog/mentions-vs-citations-why-both-matter-for-your-ai-visibility), carries more weight than any line you write about yourself, because [AI models weight independent mentions](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-ai-models-decide-which-brands-to-recommend) far above your own marketing copy.

**Owned content is what you control. Earned citations are what models trust. You need both, and you should start by finding out which pages models cite today.**

## Why earned beats owned here

A homepage tagline is a claim. A balanced review on a respected platform is evidence. Models behave like a skeptical buyer: they discount what a brand says about itself and lean on what independent sources say. That is why the off-site map below usually moves the needle more than another landing page.

It also explains why you cannot copy one playbook everywhere. Similarweb reports, from roughly 600,000 AI citation events, only about 11% domain overlap between the sources ChatGPT and Perplexity cite, and about half of cited domains changing month to month ([Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/best-ai-citation-analysis-tools/), a vendor-published figure). The sources that matter differ per model and per month, so the target list has to come from data, not from a generic list.

## The sources worth earning

Not every citation is equal, and models appear to discount obviously promotional content. Aim for credible, independent surfaces:

| Source type | Examples | Why it gets cited | Typical effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Review platforms | G2, Capterra, TrustRadius | Recent, specific customer reviews, strong in software categories | Ongoing review campaign |
| Editorial and press | Industry publications, trade media | Independent authority | Digital PR, expert contributions |
| Communities | Reddit, Hacker News, niche forums | Buyers compare notes in plain language | Genuine participation, slow |
| Reference sites | Wikipedia, Wikidata | Structured, neutral | Only if you meet the notability bar |
| Comparison pages | "Best of" and "X alternatives" pages run by others | Directly answer buying prompts | Outreach with accurate facts |

Do not fake notability on Wikipedia; the criteria are strict and gaming them backfires. Social platforms can matter more than you expect on some engines: Promptwatch reports they were 0.46% of ChatGPT citations but 12.2% of Google AI Overview citations in one week in autumn 2026, as summarised by [Orchly](https://orchly.ai/blog/best-ai-citation-tracking-tools/). Treat figures like this as a prompt to check your own data, not as a rule.

## How to actually earn them

Treat this as a targeted campaign, not scattered outreach:

1.  **Find what AI already cites for your category.** Take your 10 to 20 most important prompts and list the exact URLs cited per model. Those pages are your priority list.

2.  **Find the source gaps.** Look for cited pages that mention your competitors but not you. These are the highest-value placements because the model already trusts the page.

3.  **Rank by signal and effort.** A comparison list that appears in three prompts beats a forum thread that appears in one.

4.  **Earn the placement honestly.** Run customer review campaigns, pursue digital PR, contribute real expertise, and ask list maintainers to include you with accurate facts. A glowing affiliate post does less than a balanced write-up from a source readers and models trust.

5.  **Fix your own pages too.** Citations rarely stand alone. Clear definitions, schema markup and a crawlable site make it easier for models to reuse what others say about you. See [how to automate schema markup for AI citations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-automate-schema-markup-for-ai-citations).

A caution on scale: not every cited page is a realistic target. Ahrefs data summarised by Orchly suggests only about 32.3% of ChatGPT's top 1,000 cited pages are realistic outreach targets. Prioritise the ones where a genuine contribution is possible.

## FAQ

### What does it mean to be cited by AI?

An AI answer credits a specific URL as a source. This is different from a mention, where your brand name appears in the text without a source link. The two vary independently, so track both.

### How long does it take for a new placement to show up in AI answers?

There is no fixed timeline. It depends on the model, on whether it retrieves live sources, and on how often the page is crawled. Measure daily and compare against your baseline over several weeks before judging a placement.

### Should I buy placements or sponsored reviews?

No. Models and readers discount obviously promotional content, and platforms penalise undisclosed paid reviews. Invest in genuine customer reviews, real expertise and accurate listings instead.
