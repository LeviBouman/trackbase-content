## How we ranked these tools

Five criteria, weighted in this order:

1.  **Model coverage.** How many [AI platforms](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026) the tool actually queries, and whether that includes Google AI Overview and Copilot, which most tools skip.

2.  **Citation and source tracking.** Whether you can see which URLs the models cite, since that is where the optimization work starts.

3.  **Actionability.** Whether the tool tells you what to do next or only shows you charts.

4.  **Commerce coverage.** Whether product-level tracking exists (ChatGPT Shopping), relevant for every webshop.

5.  **Price for what you get.** Entry price against feature depth.

Disclosure: Trackbase is our platform. We put it first because it is the only tool in this list that combines 11-model coverage, [crawler analytics](https://trackbase.ai/features/crawler-analytics) and ChatGPT Shopping tracking under €100 per month. Where competitors beat us, the entry says so.

## 2\. Profound

Profound is the best-known enterprise platform in this category. Strong conversation-volume data, agent analytics and the brand recognition that comes with large customers. If you have an enterprise budget and a team to use it, it delivers.

-   **Pricing:** custom, generally reported in the hundreds of dollars per month and up

-   **Best for:** large brands and enterprises

-   **Watch outs:** pricing puts it out of reach for most SMBs. No product-level shopping tracking. See our detailed comparison in Trackbase vs Profound.

## 3\. Peec AI

Peec built a clean, focused visibility tracker that marketing teams pick up quickly. Good competitor benchmarking and a straightforward interface.

-   **Pricing:** from around €90/month

-   **Best for:** marketing teams that want fast time to value

-   **Watch outs:** thinner on sources and technical diagnostics than the tools above it.

## 4\. Otterly.ai

Otterly is the budget entry point of this market and gets cited in almost every roundup for that reason. [Prompt tracking](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking) on the major models with simple reporting.

## FAQ

### What is an AI visibility tool?

Software that repeatedly asks AI models the questions your customers ask, records whether your brand appears in the answers, and reports mentions, positions, sentiment and cited sources over time.

### Do I still need SEO tools if I track AI visibility?

Yes. Classic search still drives most traffic for most sites, and AI models lean on content that also does well in search. The two disciplines overlap but measure different outcomes.

### How much do AI visibility tools cost?

Entry-level tools start around $29 to $39 per month. Mid-market platforms sit between €79 and €300. Enterprise platforms such as Profound are custom-priced, typically several hundred per month and up.

### Which AI platforms should I monitor?

Start with ChatGPT, Google AI Overview and Gemini, since they carry the most usage. Add Perplexity, Copilot and Claude if your audience skews professional. Tracking 11 models costs the same effort as tracking three if the tool does the work.

### How often should the data refresh?

Daily. AI answers change constantly, and weekly snapshots hide the movement you are trying to measure.

### Can these tools improve my visibility, or only measure it?

Measurement is the core. The better platforms add recommendations. What actually moves your visibility is the work that follows: content that answers real questions, third-party mentions and reviews, and a technically accessible site.

### Are free AI visibility checkers reliable?

They are fine as a first snapshot. One-off checks vary between runs, so treat any single result with caution and look for trends instead.

### How long before visibility improves?

Plan for three to six months. Models refresh their sources at different speeds, and third-party content takes time to build. Anyone promising results in two weeks is selling something else.
