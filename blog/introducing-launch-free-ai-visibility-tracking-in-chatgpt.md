# Introducing Launch: Track Your Brand in ChatGPT for Free, Forever

> Launch is the new free plan from Trackbase. Track 5 prompts in ChatGPT every day for 1 brand and 1 competitor, with a Visibility Score and sentiment.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-09-25 · Updated 2026-09-25 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/introducing-launch-free-ai-visibility-tracking-in-chatgpt*

Most brands still do not know whether ChatGPT mentions them when a potential customer asks for a recommendation. A quick check by hand every now and then does not tell you much, and a full AI visibility platform can feel like a big step when you just want to know where you stand. That is why we built Launch: a free Trackbase plan that tracks your brand in ChatGPT every day, for as long as you use it.

**Launch is free forever. You track 1 brand and 1 competitor on 5 prompts in ChatGPT, with a Visibility Score, sentiment and a mentions feed in the platform.**

## What is Trackbase Launch?

Launch is a free plan, not a trial. It does not stop after 7 days and there is nothing to pay. You get the core of Trackbase: a fixed set of prompts that runs in ChatGPT every day, and a platform that turns the answers into numbers you can follow over time.

We built it for teams that want to measure their [AI visibility](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/ai-visibility) before they commit to a paid plan. Think of founders who want a first baseline, marketers who need numbers for a business case and anyone who simply wants to know whether ChatGPT knows their brand.

## What is included in Launch

-   **1 brand**: add your own brand and see whether ChatGPT mentions it.

-   **5 prompts, every day**: choose the 5 questions your customers ask ChatGPT. Trackbase runs them daily, so you see changes within days instead of months.

-   **ChatGPT**: Launch tracks the most widely used AI assistant.

-   **Visibility Score and sentiment**: one [Visibility Score](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/ai-visibility-score) for how visible your brand is, plus whether ChatGPT describes you positively, neutrally or negatively.

-   **Mentions feed**: every [mention of your brand](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/ai-brand-mention) in ChatGPT in one overview.

-   **1 competitor**: follow one competitor next to your own brand and see who ChatGPT mentions more often.

-   **Email support**: your questions go straight to our team.

## How to choose your 5 prompts

With five prompts, every prompt counts. The goal is not to cover your whole market, but to track the questions that sit closest to a buying decision.

-   **Start with buying questions**: "What is the best \[category\] for \[use case\]?" is closer to revenue than a general question about your industry.

-   **Add one comparison prompt**: "\[Your brand\] or \[competitor\], which is better for \[use case\]?" shows how ChatGPT weighs you against the alternative.

-   **Include one problem prompt**: "How do I solve \[the problem your product solves\]?" reveals whether ChatGPT suggests your type of solution at all.

-   **Write like your customers**: use the words a buyer would type, including the location or language when that matters in your market.

-   **Keep them stable**: change your prompts as little as possible, so your trend line stays comparable over time.

Our guide on [finding out whether AI recommends your products](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-do-i-know-if-ai-recommends-my-products-or-services) goes deeper into building a set of [tracked prompts](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/prompt-tracking) that holds up.

## What you can learn from Launch

Five prompts and one competitor are enough to answer the questions most teams start with:

-   Does ChatGPT mention my brand for the questions that matter to my business?

-   How does it describe my brand, and is that description correct?

-   For which prompts does a competitor show up instead of me?

-   Does my visibility move after I publish new content, get press coverage or collect reviews?

Those answers give you a baseline, and a baseline is what turns AI visibility from a gut feeling into something you can report on. Our article on [why sentiment decides who wins](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins) explains why the way ChatGPT talks about you matters as much as whether it mentions you.

## When to upgrade to Essential or Professional

Launch is useful on its own, but it is deliberately focused. You will notice the limits in these situations:

-   You want to track more prompts or more competitors.

-   Your customers also use other AI assistants, such as Gemini, Perplexity or Claude.

-   You want to see which sources AI models cite and get a list of concrete next steps.

Essential tracks 10 prompts across 3 AI models and 3 competitors. Professional covers all 11 AI models, 20 prompts and 10 competitors, plus AI Insights, sources and an action list. Both come with a 7-day free trial. You can compare everything on our [pricing page](https://trackbase.ai/pricing).
