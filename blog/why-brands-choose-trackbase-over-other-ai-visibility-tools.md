# Why Brands Choose Trackbase Over Other AI Visibility Tools

> Four criteria separate AI visibility tools: platform coverage, EU data hosting, refresh frequency and ranked actions. Here is how Trackbase compares on each.

*By Bodrik Bakker · Published 2026-06-17 · Updated 2026-11-02 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/why-brands-choose-trackbase-over-other-ai-visibility-tools*

Brands choose Trackbase over other AI visibility tools for four reasons: it tracks 11 AI platforms instead of the usual handful, it hosts all data in the EU, it refreshes every 24 hours, and it turns the data into a ranked list of actions with impact scores. If you are comparing options, those are the four criteria that separate a reporting dashboard from a tool your team can run weekly work on.

## Why the comparison criteria matter

AI visibility tools are appearing fast, and they are not built the same. Two tools can both show a "visibility score" while measuring different models, at different frequencies, with different answers to what you should do next. Before you look at price, check the four points below. They decide whether the data is complete, whether legal will sign off, whether you can see change in time, and whether the output leads to work.

## 1\. Coverage of 11 AI platforms

Many [AI visibility tools](https://trackbase.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) track a handful of platforms, usually ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. [Trackbase](https://trackbase.ai/blog/inside-the-trackbase-dashboard-track-analyze-act-in-one-view) tracks 11:

-   [ChatGPT](https://trackbase.ai/blog/track-what-chatgpt-says-about-your-company), Claude, Gemini and [Perplexity](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026)

-   Google AI Overview and Google AI Mode

-   Grok, DeepSeek, Copilot, LLaMA and Mistral

That matters because each platform pulls from different sources and surfaces different brands. Trackbase's own data shows how uneven this is: in one recent seven-day window our Visibility Score was 7.4 in ChatGPT, 4.8 in Gemini and 4.3 in Perplexity, and close to zero in several other models. A tool that tracks only four platforms would never show you the other seven. Note that the Essential plan monitors 3 AI models and the Professional plan all 11, so check which plan matches the coverage you need.

## 2\. EU data hosting and GDPR compliance

Trackbase's infrastructure is located in the Netherlands. All data is hosted within the EU, under European data-protection rules.

For European brands and any business with EU customers this is often a procurement requirement rather than a preference. A tool hosted outside the EU can add a data-transfer review to your legal process, which slows down purchase and renewal. If your security questionnaire asks where data is processed, "Netherlands, EU" is a short answer.

## 3\. Daily data refresh

Trackbase runs [prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking) across the tracked platforms every 24 hours. Some tools refresh less often, and the difference matters for three reasons:

-   AI answers change quickly, and a weekly view can miss real movement.

-   You see the effect of a content change in days instead of waiting for a monthly report.

-   A sudden drop in visibility surfaces early enough to investigate.

Daily refresh turns AI visibility from a quarterly report into an operational metric. Because single AI answers vary from run to run, daily data also lets you judge a trend instead of reacting to one odd response.

## 4\. Actionable insights with impact scores

Most tools tell you what is happening. Trackbase's [Actions](https://trackbase.ai/features/actions) also tell you what to do, and rank [recommendations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-ai-assistants-decide-which-brands-to-recommend) by predicted impact on a scale from 1 to 10:

-   which content to create or improve

-   which sources to target

-   which changes should move your visibility fastest

Each action is generated from your own tracking data (content gaps, competitor coverage, sentiment, model blind spots) rather than from a generic checklist, so a team can sequence work by expected impact instead of guesswork. We explain the full method in [how to prioritize model-specific AI visibility actions](https://trackbase.ai/blog/prioritized-model-specific-ai-visibility-actions). Actions and AI Insights are part of the Professional plan.
