> AI visibility tools are appearing fast, and they’re not all built the same. If you’re comparing options, four things actually matter: how many platforms you cover, where your data lives, how often it refreshes, and whether the tool tells you what to do next.

AI visibility tools are appearing fast, and they’re not all built the same. If you’re comparing options, four things actually matter: how many platforms you cover, where your data lives, how often it refreshes, and whether the tool tells you what to do next. Here’s how Trackbase stacks up on each.

## 1\. Coverage of 11 AI platforms

Most [AI visibility tools](https://trackbase.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) track 4 to 6 platforms: usually ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. [Trackbase](https://trackbase.ai/blog/inside-the-trackbase-dashboard-track-analyze-act-in-one-view) tracks 11:

-   [ChatGPT](https://trackbase.ai/blog/track-what-chatgpt-says-about-your-company)

-   Claude

-   Gemini

-   [Perplexity](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026)

-   Grok

-   DeepSeek

-   Copilot

-   LLaMA

-   Mistral

-   Google AI Overviews

-   Google AI Mode

That matters because each platform pulls from different sources and surfaces different brands. A tool that misses 5 platforms is missing a meaningful slice of where your buyers actually search.

## 2\. EU data hosting and GDPR compliance

Trackbase’s infrastructure is located in the Netherlands. All data is hosted within the EU, fully under European data-protection rules.

For European brands and any business with EU customers, that’s not a nice-to-have. It’s a procurement requirement. Tools hosted in the US can create compliance friction that slows down legal review and renewal.

## 3\. Daily data refresh

Trackbase runs [prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking) across all 11 platforms every 24 hours. Some tools refresh weekly or even monthly. The difference matters because:

-   AI models update quickly, a weekly view can miss real movement

-   You see the impact of content changes in days, not months

-   Sudden drops in visibility surface fast enough to act on

Daily refresh turns AI visibility from a quarterly report into an operational metric.

## 4\. Actionable insights with impact scores

Most tools tell you what’s happening. Trackbase’s Insights module also tells you what to do and ranks [recommendations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-ai-assistants-decide-which-brands-to-recommend) by predicted impact:

-   Which content to create

-   Which sources to target

-   Which changes will move the score fastest

Each item carries an impact score so marketing teams can sequence work by ROI rather than guesswork. That’s the difference between a dashboard and a roadmap.

## Plus: built for teams and agencies

Trackbase supports collaboration out of the box. You can invite teammates to a shared workspace, and the Business tier unlocks white-label and unlimited workspaces, built [for agencies](https://trackbase.ai/solutions/agency) managing multiple client brands. Many AI visibility tools were built for solo operators and creak under multi-brand use.
