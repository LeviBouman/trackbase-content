# Inside the Trackbase Platform: Track, Analyze, Act in One View

> AI visibility data is only useful if you can act on it. The Trackbase platform is built around three steps: Track, Analyze, Act. We also have 7 modules that take you from raw signal to your prioritised next move.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-06-14 · Updated 2026-10-30 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/inside-the-trackbase-dashboard-track-analyze-act-in-one-view*

The Trackbase platform turns AI visibility data into a daily Track, Analyze, Act loop: it runs your prompts across up to 11 AI models, shows where your brand is mentioned and cited, and ends in a prioritised list of actions with a predicted impact score. Seven modules cover that loop, from the Dashboard to Competitors. This guide walks through each one and explains which questions it answers.

## The Track, Analyze, Act loop

AI visibility data is only useful if you can act on it. Trackbase is therefore organised in three steps:

| Step | Question | Modules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Track | Where do AI models mention or cite us? | Dashboard, Prompt Tracking, Mentions Feed |
| Analyze | Why, on which model, and against whom? | AI Model Performance, Competitors |
| Act | What should we do next, and did it work? | Insights, Analytics |

Data refreshes every 24 hours, so a change you make on Monday can show up in the numbers within days instead of months.

## 1\. Dashboard

The home view. [Visibility Score](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-trackbase-calculates-your-visibility-score-and-why-its-different), sentiment, [Share of Voice](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-measure-ai-share-of-voice) and competitor ranking sit on one screen with trend lines. The Essential plan shows Visibility Score and sentiment; Professional adds Share of Voice and competitor ranking. This is where you start your day: one glance tells you whether your AI presence is up, flat or under pressure.

## 2\. Prompt Tracking

See every prompt that is run for your brand and category. Filter by AI model, market or topic to find where you appear and where you do not. Essential includes 10 [tracked prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking); Professional includes 20. Choose prompts the way your buyers ask questions: comparisons, "best tool for..." questions and problem-led queries, not only your brand name.

## 3\. Mentions Feed

A stream of every mention and [citation](https://trackbase.ai/blog/mentions-vs-citations-why-both-matter-for-your-ai-visibility) across the [11 AI platforms](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026) Trackbase covers. Drill into individual responses to see the exact context in which your brand appeared, and flag mentions worth acting on. The difference matters: a mention says the model knows you, a citation says it trusts one of your pages enough to link it.

## 4\. AI Model Performance

A per-platform breakdown of how you score on [ChatGPT](https://trackbase.ai/blog/track-what-chatgpt-says-about-your-company) versus Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and the rest. Models behave differently: a brand can be strong on one assistant and invisible on another, and the fix is rarely the same. This view shows exactly where your blind spots are.

## 5\. Insights

This is the "Act" half of the platform. Insights turns raw data into a prioritised to-do list:

-   which content to create or rewrite

-   which sources to target for citations

-   which changes will lift visibility fastest

Each recommendation carries a predicted impact score, so you can sequence work by expected return instead of gut feel. Technical gaps, such as pages that AI crawlers cannot reach, show up here too.

## 6\. Analytics

Deeper trend analysis with date ranges, segment views and exportable reports. Professional includes [Google Analytics](https://trackbase.ai/blog/why-you-cant-measure-ai-search-in-google-analytics-and-what-to-track-instead) and Search Console connections, so AI visibility data lives next to your other marketing metrics. Crawler Analytics adds a view of which AI crawlers visit your site, which helps explain why a page is or is not cited.

## 7\. Competitors

Track competitors next to your own brand. Essential covers 3 competitors, Professional covers 10. You see their Visibility Score, Share of Voice and [sentiment](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins) in the same views as your own, which makes [benchmarking against competitors](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-do-i-compare-ai-visibility-against-competitors) immediate.

## How the modules work together

The seven modules are designed to flow:

1.  The Dashboard tells you what is happening.

2.  Prompt Tracking and the Mentions Feed show why.

3.  AI Model Performance and Competitors show against whom and on which model.

4.  Insights tells you what to do next.

5.  Analytics measures whether it worked.

A practical weekly rhythm: check the Dashboard on Monday, open Insights and pick the top two actions, ship them during the week, and review the Mentions Feed and Analytics the following Monday.

## FAQ

### What is the Track, Analyze, Act loop in Trackbase?

It is the workflow behind the platform: track your prompts across AI models, analyse where and why you do or do not appear, then act on prioritised recommendations and measure the result.

### How often does Trackbase update its data?

Prompts are run daily and data refreshes every 24 hours, so trends are visible within days.

### Which AI models does Trackbase track?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overview, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Copilot, LLaMA and Mistral. How many are included depends on your plan.
