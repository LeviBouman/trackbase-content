# Inside the Trackbase Platform: Track, Analyze, Act in One View

> AI visibility data is only useful if you can act on it. The Trackbase platform is built around three steps: Track, Analyze, Act. We also have 7 modules that take you from raw signal to your prioritised next move.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-06-14 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/inside-the-trackbase-dashboard-track-analyze-act-in-one-view*

AI visibility data is only useful if you can act on it. The Trackbase platform is built around three steps: Track, Analyze, Act. We also have 7 modules that take you from raw signal to your prioritised next move. Here’s what each one does.

## 1\. Dashboard

The home view. [Visibility Score](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-trackbase-calculates-your-visibility-score-and-why-its-different), Sentiment, [Share of Voice](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-measure-ai-share-of-voice) and competitor ranking, all on one screen with trend lines. The Pro plan unlocks the full version with all four metrics; Starter shows Visibility Score and Sentiment.

This is where you start your day: one glance tells you whether your AI presence is up, flat or under pressure.

## 2\. Prompt Tracking

See every prompt being run for your brand and category. Filter by AI model, market or topic to find exactly where you appear and where you don’t. Starter includes 10 [tracked prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking); Pro includes 20.

## 3\. Mentions Feed

A real-time stream of every mention and [citations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/mentions-vs-citations-why-both-matter-for-your-ai-visibility) across all [11 AI platforms](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026). You can drill into individual responses, see the exact context your brand appeared in, and flag mentions worth action.

## 4\. AI Model Performance

A per-platform breakdown showing how you score on [ChatGPT](https://trackbase.ai/blog/track-what-chatgpt-says-about-your-company) versus Claude versus Perplexity and so on. This reveals exactly where you have strong AI presence and where you have blind spots.

## 5\. Insights

This is the “Act” half of the platform. Insights turns raw data into a prioritised to-do list:

-   Which content to create

-   Which sources to target

-   Which changes will boost visibility fastest

Each recommendation comes with a predicted impact score so you can sequence work by ROI, not gut feel.

## 6\. Analytics

Deeper trend analysis with date ranges, segment views and exportable reports. Pro tier includes [Google Analytics](https://trackbase.ai/blog/why-you-cant-measure-ai-search-in-google-analytics-and-what-to-track-instead) and Looker Studio integration so AI visibility data lives next to your other marketing metrics.

## 7\. Competitors

Track competitors alongside your own brand. Starter covers 3 competitors, Pro covers 10. You see their Visibility Score, Share of Voice and [sentiment](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins) in the same views as your own, making benchmarking instant.

## How the modules work together

The 7 modules are designed to flow:

-   Dashboard tells you what’s happening

-   Prompt Tracking and Mentions Feed show you why

-   AI Model Performance and [Competitors](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-do-i-compare-ai-visibility-against-competitors) show you against whom

-   Insights tells you what to do next

-   Analytics measures whether it worked

That’s the Track, Analyze, Act loop running on real data, every 24 hours.
