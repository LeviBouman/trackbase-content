# Which Tools Track AI Share of Voice Across AI Search Models?

> Yes, several tools track brand visibility and share of voice across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI. Here is what separates them: model coverage, prompt design, mentions vs. citations and cadence.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-11-20 · Updated 2026-11-20 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/which-tools-track-ai-share-of-voice-across-ai-search-models*

Yes, several tools track brand visibility and share of voice across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI features, from free graders to enterprise suites. They differ less in the headline number than in how that number is built: which prompts, which models, how often, and whether mentions and citations are counted separately. Pick a tool on those four points, not on the dashboard.

## What "AI share of voice" actually measures

[AI share of voice](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/ai-share-of-voice) (SoV) is the share of AI answers, across a fixed set of prompts and models, in which your brand appears compared with your competitors. The simplest version is a ratio: AI responses that mention your brand divided by all responses in the [prompt](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/prompt) set, times 100. HubSpot's [share of voice guide](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/share-of-voice-tools) uses exactly that formula for its AI variant.

Treat it as a directional metric. AI answers vary by session and platform, so a single run proves little; the trend over repeated, daily samples is what you can act on. Slate makes the same point in its [roundup of AI share of voice tools](https://slatehq.com/blog/best-ai-share-of-voice-tools), calling the metric directional rather than absolute.

## The four things that decide whether a tool is any good

1.  **Model coverage.** A tool that only queries one assistant tells you about one assistant. Buyers use several, and Trackbase data shows visibility differs sharply per model: over the last 7 days our own tracked prompts scored 6.3 in ChatGPT, 11.2 in Perplexity and 5.4 in Gemini, while Claude and Copilot scored 0 on the same set.

2.  **Prompt design.** SoV is only as good as the prompt set. Slate suggests 50-100 prompts for a focused B2B product and 100-300 for several products; HubSpot recommends 100-200 for a balanced set and refreshing it at least quarterly.

3.  **Mentions versus citations.** A mention means the model names you. A citation means it uses your page as a source. A tool that merges the two hides whether you are recommended or merely referenced. See [mentions vs. citations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/mentions-vs-citations-why-both-matter-for-your-ai-visibility) for why both matter.

4.  **Cadence and competitor set.** Weekly snapshots miss volatility. Daily runs and a named competitor list make the share a real benchmark rather than a vanity number.

## A 30-minute way to compare tools yourself

You do not need a procurement process. Do this:

-   Write 20 prompts your buyers really ask, in the language they use.

-   List 3-5 competitors you are measured against.

-   Run the same set in two or three tools, or in free graders first.

-   Compare: do the tools agree on direction? Do they separate mentions from citations? Can you see which sources drive the answer?

-   Re-run a week later. A tool whose numbers swing wildly with no explanation is not ready to report to a board.

Then decide on cadence and cost. The [how to measure AI share of voice](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-measure-ai-share-of-voice) guide walks through the calculation step by step.

## Common mistakes when reading the number

-   **Changing the prompt set mid-stream.** If prompts change, the share changes for the wrong reason. Version your set and note every edit.

-   **Comparing models as if they were one.** A 10% share in Perplexity and a 2% share in Gemini are two separate stories with different fixes.

-   **Ignoring sentiment.** Being named often but described poorly is not a win. Read [why sentiment decides who wins](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins) before celebrating a rising share.

## FAQ

**Which tool is best for tracking AI share of voice?**There is no single winner. Enterprise teams often choose Profound or Conductor, SEO teams extend Semrush or Ahrefs, and teams that want daily multi-model tracking with actions pick a dedicated platform such as Trackbase. Match the tool to your model coverage, prompt volume and budget.

**Can I track AI share of voice for free?**Partly. Free graders give a one-off baseline across a handful of prompts. For a trend you need repeated runs, which usually means a paid plan or a free trial; Trackbase offers 14 days.

**How many prompts do I need for a reliable number?**Public guidance ranges from 50-100 prompts for a focused product up to 100-300 for several products. Start smaller if you must, but keep the set fixed so changes in share reflect real movement rather than a changed prompt list.
