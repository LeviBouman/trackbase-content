# Free and Low-Cost AI Visibility Trackers for Small Webshops

> Which free checkers and low-cost trackers work for small e-commerce teams, what they cost, and how to pick the smallest plan that gives real insight.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-11-09 · Updated 2026-11-09 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/free-and-low-cost-ai-visibility-trackers-for-small-ecommerce-teams*

The best free or low-cost setup for a small e-commerce team has two layers: a free checker for a one-time baseline, and a paid tracker of roughly $20–€50 per month for daily monitoring. Free checkers show whether AI models mention your shop today; only a tracker that reruns the same prompts shows whether that changes. Start free, then pay for the smallest plan that covers your real customer prompts.

## Free checkers vs. low-cost trackers: what you actually get

The two categories answer different questions, and mixing them up is the most common budget mistake.

-   **A free checker** runs a handful of prompts once and returns a snapshot. It answers: "Do AI models know my brand at all?"

-   **A tracker** reruns a fixed [prompt](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/prompt) set on a schedule and stores the history. It answers: "Are we gaining or losing ground, and against whom?"

Ahrefs' free [AI Visibility](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/ai-visibility) Checker is a good example of the first category. According to its own page, it needs no signup, covers ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, [Google AI Overviews](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/google-ai-overviews) and [Google AI Mode](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/google-ai-mode), and shows the top 5 topics, cited domains and cited pages. It is explicitly a one-time snapshot: no historical trends, no share of voice, no competitor comparison and no custom prompts.

AI answers are also unstable. A SparkToro study of 2,961 runs, as summarised by Brand24, found the same brand list in the same order about once in 1,000 runs. One snapshot is therefore a data point, not a measurement. That is why a baseline alone is never enough.

The headline price tells you little. Compare three numbers instead: prompts included, models covered, and how often prompts run. A $20 plan that runs 15 prompts weekly can be worse than a €49 plan that runs 10 prompts daily, depending on how fast your category changes.

## How to choose the smallest plan that works

Small shops rarely need 200 prompts. They need the 10 to 20 questions that decide a purchase. Brand24's guide recommends 20 to 50 prompts across five prompt types and changing the set no more than once a quarter, which is a sensible ceiling for a small team.

1.  **Write your prompts from customer language.** Use "best running shoes for flat feet under €150" rather than "AI visibility for Brand X".

2.  **Split them across intents:** category discovery, comparison, price or budget, local or Dutch-language, and brand-specific questions.

3.  **Pick models by where your buyers are.** For most webshops that means ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity first.

4.  **Freeze the set for a quarter.** Changing prompts mid-stream breaks your trend line.

5.  **Add competitors you actually lose sales to**, not just the biggest brands.

## Why e-commerce teams should care now

AI assistants are becoming a product-discovery channel. Adobe Analytics, cited by Brand24, reported AI referrals to US retail sites up 138% year over year in May 2026, converting 54% better than non-AI traffic. Pew Research found that users click a result 8% of the time when an AI Overview appears, versus 15% without one. The click you used to win from a ranking is increasingly decided inside the answer.

That makes the [cited sources](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/cited-sources) the real battleground. In our own tracked prompts, answers about tools for small e-commerce teams cite a mix of vendor blogs, comparison roundups and free-checker pages. A small shop can influence this by being listed accurately on the pages models already cite, and by keeping its own product and FAQ pages clear and consistent.

## A simple month-one plan

-   **Week 1:** run two free checkers and write down mentions, cited pages and wording.

-   **Week 2:** build a 10–20 prompt set and start a free trial of one tracker.

-   **Week 3:** compare results against two or three competitors; list the cited pages where you are missing.

-   **Week 4:** fix one thing (a product page, a comparison listing, a missing FAQ) and watch the next week's runs.

If you sell through ChatGPT Shopping or run ads there, check that your plan covers it: on Trackbase, ChatGPT Shopping tracking is a Professional add-on.

## FAQ

### Is a free AI visibility checker enough for a small webshop?

For a first baseline, yes. For decisions, no. Free checkers give a one-time snapshot, and AI answers vary from run to run, so you need repeated runs of the same prompts to see a trend.

### How many prompts should a small e-commerce team track?

Start with 10 to 20 prompts that mirror real purchase questions, across discovery, comparison, price and Dutch-language variants. Increase only when you have acted on what the first set showed.

### What is the cheapest way to start with daily tracking?

Run free checkers first, then take a 14-day trial of one tracker. Trackbase Essential at €49 per month covers 10 prompts across 3 models; cheaper tools exist from about $20, with fewer models or less frequent runs, so compare prompts, models and run frequency, not only price.
