# 9 Best Profound Alternatives for AI Visibility Tracking (2026)

> Profound is built for enterprise budgets. These 9 alternatives track your brand in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity from 29 to 300 euros per month. Compared honestly.

*By Bodrik Bakker · Published 2026-07-14 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/best-profound-alternatives*

Profound more or less defined the AI visibility category, and for enterprise teams it remains a strong product. It is also custom-priced at a level most companies cannot justify, which is why "Profound alternatives" became a search of its own. We build one of those alternatives, so the disclosure is right here at the top: Trackbase is ours, it opens this list, and every entry says what Profound still does better.

## Why teams look for an alternative

Three reasons come back in almost every conversation we have:

1.  **Price.** [Profound](https://trackbase.ai/blog/trackbase-vs-profound) is sales-led with custom quotes, generally reported at several hundred dollars per month and up. Mid-market budgets stop earlier.

2.  **Overkill.** Conversation-volume datasets and agent analytics are impressive, but a 15-person [e-commerce](https://trackbase.ai/solutions/e-commerce) team mostly needs to know where it stands and what to fix this week.

3.  **Product tracking.** Webshops want to see their products inside [ChatGPT Shopping](https://trackbase.ai/features/chatgpt-shopping), and that is not what Profound was built around.

## 2\. Peec AI

**What it is:** a focused visibility tracker with clean [competitor benchmarking](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-do-i-compare-ai-visibility-against-competitors), popular with European marketing teams.

-   **Pricing:** from around €90/month

-   **What sets it apart:** speed to first insight; teams are running within an hour.

-   **Limitations:** lighter on source diagnostics and technical signals.

**Versus Profound:** covers the everyday tracking need at a tenth of the budget, without the enterprise analytics layer.

## 3\. Otterly.ai

**What it is:** the budget entry of the category, [tracking prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking) on the major models with simple reports.

-   **Pricing:** from around $29/month

-   **What sets it apart:** lowest barrier to entry in the market.

-   **Limitations:** depth. [Sentiment](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins), sources and larger prompt sets are where it runs out.

**Versus Profound:** not comparable in scope, and not trying to be. It answers "am I visible at all" for the price of a lunch.

## 4\. Scrunch AI

**What it is:** mid-market to enterprise platform with an angle on how AI agents experience your site.

-   **Pricing:** custom

-   **What sets it apart:** agent-readiness perspective next to answer tracking.

-   **Limitations:** opaque pricing, sales-led motion.

**Versus Profound:** the closest philosophical match on this list. If you want Profound positioning with a different vendor, start here.

## 5\. Ahrefs Brand Radar

**What it is:** the Ahrefs AI visibility module, built on a very large prompt dataset derived from real search behavior.

-   **Pricing:** part of Ahrefs plans

-   **What sets it apart:** dataset scale, and zero extra procurement if you already run Ahrefs.

-   **Limitations:** a module inside an SEO suite, with the priorities of an SEO suite.

**Versus Profound:** strong on data breadth, weaker on dedicated workflows. For existing Ahrefs customers it is the obvious first step before buying anything new.

## 6\. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

**What it is:** the Semrush AI toolkit covering visibility, perception and narrative analysis, on top of a 200-million-plus prompt database.

-   **Pricing:** add-on within Semrush One

-   **What sets it apart:** polish and reporting; the perception reports are genuinely useful for [brand teams](https://trackbase.ai/solutions/pr-brand-teams).

-   **Limitations:** total cost on top of an existing subscription climbs quickly.

**Versus Profound:** the most credible big-vendor alternative, at a lower but not low price point.

## 7\. SE Ranking AI Visibility Tracker

**What it is:** AI tracking inside the SE Ranking rank tracker, with a free daily check as the entry point.

-   **Pricing:** add-on to SE Ranking plans

-   **What sets it apart:** the free checker, and familiar UX for SEO teams.

-   **Limitations:** narrower model coverage than dedicated platforms.

**Versus Profound:** a pragmatic budget route for SEO teams, not an enterprise replacement.

## 8\. Rankscale.ai

**What it is:** GEO analysis with per-engine visibility scoring.

-   **Pricing:** trial plus tiered plans

-   **What sets it apart:** clear per-engine breakdowns.

-   **Limitations:** smaller action layer and ecosystem.

**Versus Profound:** covers scoring, not the strategic analytics.

## 9\. Nightwatch

**What it is:** classic SERP rank tracking and LLM monitoring in one subscription.

-   **Pricing:** from $32/month

-   **What sets it apart:** consolidation; one tool for SEO positions and [AI answers](https://trackbase.ai/blog/monitor-changes-in-ai-answers-over-time).

-   **Limitations:** the AI side is newer than the rank tracker.

**Versus Profound:** different weight class, useful if consolidation beats depth for you.

## FAQ

### Why is Profound so expensive?

It sells conversation-volume data, agent analytics and enterprise service alongside tracking, with a sales-led model. For the teams that use all of it, the price is defensible. Most teams use a fraction of it.

### Is there a free Profound alternative?

Free checkers exist, including ours, and manual spreadsheet tracking costs nothing but time. For continuous multi-model tracking there is no serious free option; entry-level paid tools start around $29 per month.

### What does Profound do that cheaper tools do not?

Conversation-volume estimates (how often prompts are actually asked), agent analytics, and the enterprise layer: SSO, procurement support, account teams. The core loop of tracking, sources and recommendations is available well below its price point.

### Which alternative covers the most AI platforms?

Trackbase tracks 11 models, which is the widest coverage on this list. Most alternatives cover four to six.

### Can I switch tools without losing my history?

Your baseline restarts with a new tool, since each platform runs its own prompts. Keep exports from your old tool, run both in parallel for a couple of weeks, and accept a short overlap cost. Prompt sets transfer easily; historical scores do not.
