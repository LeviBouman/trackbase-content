# AEO for Dutch Marketers: How It Differs From SEO

> AEO earns the mention inside an AI answer, SEO earns the click. Here is what changes for Dutch marketers, with a five-step playbook and the metrics to track.

*By Bodrik Bakker · Published 2026-11-13 · Updated 2026-11-13 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/aeo-for-dutch-marketers-how-answer-engine-optimization-differs-from-seo*

[Answer engine optimization](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/answer-engine-optimization) (AEO) is the practice of making your brand the source that AI assistants quote, cite or recommend in their answers, while SEO is the practice of ranking a page in a list of search results. For Dutch marketers the difference is practical: SEO earns the click, AEO earns the mention inside an answer that is often given in Dutch, to a Dutch buyer, without a click at all. You still need SEO, because answer engines lean on pages that already rank, but you need a second playbook and a second scoreboard next to it.

## AEO and SEO in one table

|  | SEO | AEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal | Rank higher, earn the click | Be the source an [answer engine](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/answer-engine) trusts and cites |
| Unit of work | A page targeting a keyword | A clear answer to a question, backed by entity signals |
| Typical query | Short keywords ("crm software") | Full questions ("welk crm past bij een mkb in Nederland?") |
| Success looks like | Position, CTR, sessions | Mentions, citations, Share of Voice, sentiment |
| Where it happens | Google and Bing results pages | ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, [Google AI Mode](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/google-ai-mode), Copilot and others |
| Feedback loop | Weekly rank checks | Daily [prompt](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/prompt) runs, because answers change between runs |

HubSpot's explainer makes the same point: AEO does not replace SEO, it builds on it, and many answer engines draw from pages that already rank organically ([HubSpot on AEO](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/answer-engine-optimization)). If you want the wider SEO, AEO and [GEO](https://trackbase.ai/glossary/generative-engine-optimization) split, our earlier piece covers it: [AEO vs GEO vs SEO: what's the difference](https://trackbase.ai/blog/aeo-vs-geo-vs-seo-whats-the-difference).

## Why this matters more once discovery stops producing clicks

The click is exactly what shrinks first. Pew Research found that users clicked a traditional result only 8% of the time when Google showed an AI summary, against 15% when it did not (as cited by [HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/aeo-vs-seo)). HubSpot also cites a Search Engine Land study that put nearly 60% of Google queries at zero clicks in 2024.

The traffic that does arrive from AI answers is not worthless. HubSpot reports a 11.4% conversion rate for AI referral traffic against 5.3% for organic search ([source](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/answer-engine-optimization)). Fewer visits, higher intent: you cannot manage that with a rank tracker alone.

## What changes for Dutch marketers specifically

Most AEO advice is written in English for the US. Four things differ when your buyers ask in Dutch.

1.  **Prompts are full sentences in Dutch.** Buyers type "welke tool volgt of ChatGPT mijn merk noemt?" rather than a keyword. Your prompt set has to mirror that, including Dutch spelling and Dutch product terms.

2.  **The source pool is different.** For Dutch prompts, models cite Dutch publishers, price comparison sites, review platforms and .nl brand pages next to the usual global names. Winning the English answer does not mean you show up in the Dutch one.

3.  **Sentiment can differ per language.** The same brand can be described warmly in English and neutrally in Dutch, depending on which sources a model reads. See [how to track brand sentiment in Dutch AI answers](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-track-brand-sentiment-in-dutch-ai-answers).

4.  **Your reporting must be Dutch-ready.** Marketing teams in the Netherlands often report to a management team that wants one number per model; see [AI visibility metrics for Dutch marketing teams](https://trackbase.ai/blog/ai-visibility-metrics-for-dutch-marketing-teams).

## The AEO playbook in five steps

1.  **Start from SEO foundations.** Crawlable pages, fast, with clean internal linking. Gemini in particular tends to draw from pages ranking within the top 20 organic results, according to HubSpot.

2.  **Write the answer first.** Use the question as an H2, answer it in two sentences, then add depth. If the answer needs more than two sentences, put the detail below it.

3.  **Add structured data.** FAQ, How-To and Article schema make the structure explicit to machines.

4.  **Strengthen entity signals.** A clear About page, named authors, consistent company details and credible third-party mentions tell a model who you are and why to trust you.

5.  **Earn mentions off-site.** Answer engines judge a brand across the web, so reviews, comparison pages and trade press matter as much as your own blog.

## How to measure AEO

A rank tracker cannot tell you whether ChatGPT names you. The AEO scoreboard has these lines:

-   **Mentions:** does the brand appear in the answer to a tracked prompt?

-   **Citations:** is one of your pages listed as a source?

-   **Share of Voice:** your share of mentions against competitors across the same prompts.

-   **Sentiment:** how the answer describes you.

-   **Position:** where in the answer you appear.

## A realistic first month

-   **Week 1:** write 10 to 15 real buyer questions in Dutch and English, one per decision stage.

-   **Week 2:** record the baseline: which models mention you, which sources they cite instead.

-   **Week 3:** rewrite the five pages closest to those questions answer-first, add FAQ schema.

-   **Week 4:** re-measure and compare against the baseline, then pick the next five pages.

Small teams should resist covering everything at once. A handful of well-answered questions that you can measure beats a site-wide rewrite you cannot attribute.

## FAQ

### Does AEO replace SEO for Dutch websites?

No. Answer engines frequently pull from pages that already rank, so SEO remains the foundation. AEO adds answer-first content, entity signals and a different set of metrics on top.

### Do I need separate content for Dutch and English AEO?

Yes, in practice. Models answer Dutch prompts with a different source pool, so a strong English page does not guarantee a mention in the Dutch answer. Write natural Dutch content, not a literal translation, and track Dutch prompts separately.

### How do I know whether AEO is working?

Track mentions, citations, Share of Voice and sentiment for a fixed set of prompts over time, and watch branded search and AI referral traffic as secondary signals. Compare against a baseline you recorded before changing any content.
