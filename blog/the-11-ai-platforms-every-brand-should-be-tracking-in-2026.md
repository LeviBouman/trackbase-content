# Top 11 AI Platforms Every Brand Should Be Tracking in 2026

> Your customers aren’t just asking ChatGPT anymore. They’re asking Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok and a growing list of AI engines that all generate answers differently.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-06-05 · Updated 2026-10-24 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026*

Every brand should track at least the 11 AI platforms that answer buying and research questions today: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, LLaMA, Mistral, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Copilot. Each model draws on different data, weighs sources differently and names different brands for the same question, so a result on one platform tells you little about the others. Here is what each platform is, why it matters and how to decide where to start.

| Claude | Anthropic | Chat assistant | Strong with enterprise users and developers |

| Google AI Mode | Google | Conversational search | Sits between classic Google and Gemini |
| Copilot | Microsoft | Assistant in Bing, Windows, Office | Default AI at many workplaces |

## Conversational AI models

These are chat-first platforms where people put a question to the model directly.

-   **ChatGPT** is OpenAI's flagship and, for most brands, the first place to look. It is used for consumer questions, B2B research and content workflows. It also has shopping and advertising surfaces, which Trackbase monitors separately.

-   **Claude** from Anthropic is popular with enterprise teams and developers and tends to give long, structured answers.

-   **Gemini** is Google's assistant, integrated into Android and Workspace, so it has reach inside an existing user base.

-   **Grok** from xAI is built into X and is often asked about trending and real-time topics.

-   **DeepSeek** is a leading open model from China that is increasingly used in technical contexts.

-   **LLaMA** is Meta's open-weights model family. It sits behind many third-party assistants and enterprise deployments.

-   **Mistral** is a European model with a following among privacy-conscious organisations.

## AI search and discovery platforms

These blend search with generated answers and often show their sources.

-   **Perplexity** is an AI search engine that cites sources by default, which makes it useful for seeing exactly which pages shape an answer.

-   **Google AI Overviews** is the generated summary at the top of many Google results.

-   **Google AI Mode** is a deeper conversational search experience.

-   **Copilot** is Microsoft's assistant, built into Bing, Windows, Office and Edge. For enterprise buyers it is often the AI they have available at work.

## Why tracking just one is not enough

Platforms differ in training data, in how they retrieve and rank sources, and in how they present brands. A brand that is named first on Perplexity can be absent on Gemini. A B2B software company that does well in ChatGPT may not appear in Copilot, even though Copilot is what its enterprise buyers use all day. For a closer look at the mechanics, read [how AI models decide which brands to recommend](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-ai-models-decide-which-brands-to-recommend).

Tracking only the assistant you personally use creates a blind spot the size of your audience. Customers do not announce which model they asked, so the only safe approach is to measure the whole set. The [mentions-versus-citations distinction](https://trackbase.ai/blog/mentions-vs-citations-why-both-matter-for-your-ai-visibility) also behaves differently per platform: some name your brand without linking to you, others cite your page without naming you.

## How to decide where to start

If you cannot track everything on day one, a sensible order is:

1.  **Where your customers already are.** Ask sales and support which assistants customers mention.

2.  **Where your category is decided.** Comparison and "best tools" questions are often answered by ChatGPT, Perplexity and the Google surfaces.

3.  **Where your buyers work.** Enterprise audiences often have Copilot or Gemini by default.

4.  **Where you can see sources.** Perplexity and the Google AI surfaces make it easiest to learn which pages to improve.

## FAQ

### Which AI platforms matter most for my brand?

It depends on your audience. ChatGPT is the broadest, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews matter for research and comparison questions, and Copilot or Gemini matter if your buyers work inside Microsoft or Google environments. Track all 11 if you can; otherwise start with where your customers actually ask.

### Do I need to track the open models such as LLaMA, Mistral and DeepSeek?

They often sit behind other products, so brands that appear in them are usually well documented across the web. They are cheap signals of how consistently your brand is described, and they matter for technical and privacy-focused audiences.

### How often should I measure AI visibility?

Answers change from day to day, so a daily run gives a more reliable trend than a monthly spot check. Trackbase refreshes its data every 24 hours.
