# Trackbase vs Profound: Which AI Visibility Platform Should You Choose?

> Comparing Trackbase and Profound for AI visibility tracking: pricing, competitor analysis, sentiment and which platform fits smaller teams and agencies best.

*By Bodrik Bakker · Published 2026-09-10 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/trackbase-vs-profound-which-ai-visibility-platform-should-you-choose*

If you have started shopping for an AI visibility platform, you have almost certainly landed on a page comparing tools to Profound. It is the name most enterprise buyers hear first, and its pricing and reporting-first model shape how the whole category gets evaluated.

**Profound is built for enterprise reporting;** [**Trackbase**](https://trackbase.ai/features) **is built for teams that want daily, actionable visibility tracking without an enterprise contract.**

## What does Profound actually do well?

[Profound](https://trackbase.ai/blog/trackbase-vs-profound) gives large organisations deep analytics, competitor monitoring, and polished enterprise reporting across major AI platforms. For a marketing team that already has an analytics function and needs another dashboard to feed into it, that is a real strength.

The tradeoff shows up fast for smaller and mid-sized teams: pricing scales with multi-model tracking, and much of the platform's depth sits behind higher tiers or custom enterprise deals. You often need a sales conversation before you know what you are actually buying.

## Where does Trackbase differ from Profound?

Trackbase tracks brand mentions, citations, sentiment, and [Share of Voice](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-measure-ai-share-of-voice) across the models buyers actually use — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode — with a daily refresh instead of a slower reporting cadence.

The bigger difference is access, not just price. [Trackbase's core tracking, competitor benchmarking, and sentiment analysis](https://trackbase.ai/blog/why-brands-choose-trackbase-over-other-ai-visibility-tools) are available on self-service plans, so a solo marketer or a small agency can start monitoring today without a procurement cycle.

## Which platform is better for competitor analysis?

Both platforms show you who is winning the prompts you care about, but they answer different questions. Profound's competitor reporting is built for board-level summaries: who is ahead, by how much, over what period.

Trackbase is built to answer the operational question: **which specific sources are feeding the answers where a competitor beats you**, so you know exactly what to fix. That distinction matters because visibility scores alone do not tell you what action to take next — you need the citation trail behind the score.

## Does pricing actually matter for this decision?

Yes, because pricing structure changes what you can afford to track. Profound's enterprise-first pricing makes sense if you are already running a large martech stack and multiple brands, but it prices out teams that want to track dozens of prompts across several models without a five-figure annual commitment.

Trackbase's plans are built around the reality that most teams do not need an enterprise contract to get daily, multi-model tracking. You can start seeing your [Visibility Score](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-trackbase-calculates-your-visibility-score-and-why-its-different) and Share of Voice the same day you sign up, on a plan sized for your actual prompt volume rather than a seat-based enterprise minimum.

## Which tool fits a smaller marketing team or agency better?

If you are a solo marketer, an in-house team without a dedicated AI-search budget, or an agency managing several [client brands](https://trackbase.ai/solutions/agency), Trackbase is the more practical starting point. Self-service onboarding, daily tracking, and transparent pricing mean you are measuring your AI visibility this week, not after a sales cycle.

Agencies specifically benefit from [packaging AI visibility as a retainer service](https://trackbase.ai/blog/ai-visibility-for-agencies-how-to-offer-geo-tracking-as-a-service), something that is far easier to do profitably on a platform priced for multi-client use than on an enterprise-only tool.

## What should you actually track once you pick a platform?

Whichever tool you choose, the metrics that matter are the same: Visibility Score, Share of Voice, citation count and [sentiment](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins), tracked per model and per prompt category, not as a single blended number.

A platform is only as useful as the actions it points you toward. Look for the ability to see not just *whether* you were mentioned, but *which sources* the model relied on and *how it described you* — that is the difference between a report you read once and a workflow you run every week.

## The bottom line

Profound remains a strong choice if you need enterprise-scale reporting and already have the budget and procurement process for it. For most SaaS teams, agencies and growing brands, **Trackbase delivers the same daily visibility, competitor and sentiment tracking without the enterprise price tag or the sales cycle**.
