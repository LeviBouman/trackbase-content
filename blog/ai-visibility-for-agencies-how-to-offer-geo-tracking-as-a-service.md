# AI Visibility for Agencies: Offer GEO Tracking as a Service

> How agencies package AI visibility tracking as a retainer: what to sell, what to demand from the tool for multi-client work, and how to price it.

*By Bodrik Bakker · Published 2026-07-08 · Updated 2026-11-18 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/ai-visibility-for-agencies-how-to-offer-geo-tracking-as-a-service*

Agencies can offer AI visibility as a recurring retainer: a baseline audit, daily tracking across AI models for every client, white-label monthly reporting and a prioritized list of fixes. The service works when one platform monitors many clients in separate workspaces, so the margin comes from the tooling rather than from manual screenshots. This guide covers what to sell, what to demand from the tool underneath, and how to price it.

## Why this is an agency opportunity right now

Clients are starting to ask why ChatGPT or Gemini does not recommend them. Most brands know AI search matters but have no idea where they stand, and few agencies have packaged a credible answer. That gap is the opening. It is also naturally recurring work: a baseline audit, ongoing tracking, monthly reporting and continuous optimisation make a retainer rather than a one-off project.

The prompts agencies themselves ask AI models show the same shift. In Trackbase's own citation data for the last 30 days, the prompt "What software can agencies use to monitor AI answer rankings for multiple clients?" produced 1,533 citations, and the most-cited sources were vendor pages such as Rankscale (49), Peec AI (39), Profound (35) and Otterly (30). Buyers are already comparing tools; your job is to be the agency that has chosen one and built a service on it.

## What an AI visibility service includes

A complete offer has four parts, and they map onto work agencies already do:

-   **Baseline audit:** where the client appears across [AI platforms](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026) today, and against which competitors. The [free AI visibility checker](https://trackbase.ai/free-ai-visibility-checker) is a low-friction way to start the conversation.

-   **Ongoing tracking:** Visibility Score, [Share of Voice](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-measure-ai-share-of-voice), sentiment and competitor movement, refreshed daily.

-   **Optimisation:** the owned-content and earned-citation work from the [AI visibility playbook](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-improve-your-ai-visibility-a-practical-playbook) and the [guide to earning citations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-earning-mentions-on-the-sources-models-trust).

-   **Reporting:** a clear monthly narrative a client can understand and act on, under your agency's brand.

## What to demand from the tool you run it on

The platform underneath decides how profitably you can deliver the service. Five requirements matter most for multi-client work:

| Requirement | Why it matters for an agency |
| --- | --- |
| Separate workspace per client | Keeps prompts, competitors and data clean and avoids cross-client mistakes |
| White-label reporting | The deliverable carries your brand, not the vendor's |
| Model coverage | Clients' buyers use different assistants; coverage should not be the bottleneck |
| Daily refresh | Reports reflect reality, and a drop can be explained within days |
| Actionable recommendations | Findings turn directly into billable client work |

Our checklist for [white-label AI visibility dashboards](https://trackbase.ai/blog/white-label-ai-visibility-dashboards-for-agencies-checklist) goes deeper on the reporting side, and the piece on [what enterprise teams should require from an AI monitoring platform](https://trackbase.ai/blog/what-features-should-enterprise-teams-require-from-an-ai-monitoring-platform) applies to larger agency set-ups too.

## How to package and price it

1.  **Productise it.** Tier by the number of [tracked prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking), competitors or markets, so scope is clear and upsells are obvious. A common shape is a small tier with a handful of core prompts and a larger one with a full prompt set and a competitor review.

2.  **Bundle it** as the next layer on existing SEO and content retainers, not a separate sell. It is the logical extension of work you already deliver.

3.  **Lead with the audit.** A baseline report is a low-friction entry point that makes the gap concrete and the retainer easy to justify.

4.  **Report on movement, not just position.** Clients value a clear story: what changed this month, why, and what you did about it. The [actions list](https://trackbase.ai/features/actions) gives you that next step ready-made.

5.  **Set expectations.** AI answers vary between runs, so agree up front that you report trends over days and weeks rather than single answers.

## Common mistakes to avoid

-   Selling a guarantee of "ranking #1 in ChatGPT". Answers vary; sell measurement, improvement and reporting.

-   Tracking too many prompts too early. Start with the ten to twenty prompts that map to the client's revenue.

-   Reporting one model only. A drop on one assistant while another is flat is a different problem, as we explain in [model-by-model reporting](https://trackbase.ai/blog/model-by-model-ai-visibility-reporting-real-time-or-daily).

-   Ignoring sentiment. Being mentioned negatively can be worse than not being mentioned, see [why sentiment decides who wins](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins).

## FAQ

### What do agencies need to offer AI visibility as a service?

A baseline audit process, a platform that tracks AI answers daily per client, a reporting template under your own brand and a way to turn findings into content and citation work. The platform is the biggest lever because it decides how much manual work each client costs.

### Can one platform manage multiple clients?

Yes, if it supports separate workspaces per brand. On Trackbase, multiple brands and workspaces are part of the Enterprise / Agency plan; the self-serve plans cover one brand each.

### How should an agency price AI visibility tracking?

As a tiered retainer based on the number of prompts, competitors and markets, optionally bundled with existing SEO and content work. Start with a paid or free baseline audit, then convert it into monthly tracking and optimisation.
