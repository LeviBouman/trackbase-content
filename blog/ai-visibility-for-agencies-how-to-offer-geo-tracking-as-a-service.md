# AI Visibility for Agencies: How to Offer GEO Tracking as a Service

> Clients will soon ask why ChatGPT doesn't recommend them. Learn how agencies package AI visibility and GEO tracking as a recurring retainer service.

*By Bodrik Bakker · Published 2026-07-08 · Updated 2026-07-08 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/ai-visibility-for-agencies-how-to-offer-geo-tracking-as-a-service*

Your clients are about to start asking why they do not show up when someone asks ChatGPT for the best provider in their category. The agencies that have a clear answer, and a service to back it, will win those conversations. The ones that shrug will lose retainers to the ones that do not.

**AI visibility is shaping up to be the next line item on the retainer. Early movers get to define it.**

## Why this is an agency opportunity right now

Demand is forming faster than supply. Most brands know AI search matters but have no idea where they stand, and very few agencies have packaged a credible answer yet. That gap is the opening. It is also naturally recurring work: a baseline audit, ongoing tracking, monthly reporting, and continuous optimisation is a retainer, not a one-off project.

## What an AI visibility service includes

A complete offer has four parts, and they map cleanly onto work agencies already do:

-   **Baseline audit**: where the client appears across [AI platforms](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026) today, and against which competitors.

-   **Ongoing tracking**: Visibility Score, [Share of Voice](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-measure-ai-share-of-voice), sentiment, and competitor movement over time.

-   **Optimisation**: the owned-content and earned-citation work from the [AI visibility playbook](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-improve-your-ai-visibility-a-practical-playbook) and the [guide to earning citations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-earning-mentions-on-the-sources-models-trust).

-   **Reporting**: a clear monthly narrative a client can actually understand and act on.

## What to look for in the tool you run it on

The platform underneath your service decides how profitably you can deliver it. Four things matter most for agency use:

-   Multi-client management, so each brand lives in its own clean workspace.

-   White-label reporting, so the work carries your agency’s brand, not the vendor’s.

-   Broad platform coverage and frequent refresh, so your reports reflect reality rather than a stale snapshot.

-   Actionable [recommendations](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-ai-assistants-decide-which-brands-to-recommend) you can turn directly into billable client work.

This is where tooling built for solo operators tends to creak. ## How to package and price it

-   Productise it. Tier by the number of [tracked prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking), competitors, or markets, so scope is clear and upsells are obvious.

-   Bundle it as the next layer on existing SEO and content retainers, not a separate sell. It is the logical extension of work you already deliver.

-   Lead with the audit. A baseline report is a low-friction entry point that makes the gap concrete and the retainer easy to justify.
