# How Trackbase Calculates Visibility Score & Its Differences

> Most AI-tracking tools count mentions. Trackbase measures something more useful: a Visibility Score that combines presence, positioning, frequency, sentiment and competitive performance into one number you can act on.

*By Levi Bouman · Published 2026-06-11 · Original article: https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-trackbase-calculates-your-visibility-score-and-why-its-different*

Most AI-tracking tools count mentions. Trackbase measures something more useful: a Visibility Score that combines presence, positioning, frequency, sentiment and competitive performance into one number you can act on. Here’s how it works and why it tells you more than a simple mention count.

## What is the Trackbase Visibility Score?

The Visibility Score is a single, standardized metric of how often and how prominently your brand appears in AI-generated answers across the 11 platforms [Trackbase](https://trackbase.ai/blog/inside-the-trackbase-dashboard-track-analyze-act-in-one-view) monitors. It’s calculated daily so you can see exactly how visibility shifts over time and which actions move the number.

## The 5 factors behind the score

[Trackbase](https://trackbase.ai/features) analyses each AI-response on 5 dimensions:

**1\. Presence**: Was your brand mentioned at all? This is the binary baseline and the floor most other tools stop at.

**2\. Positioning**: Where in the answer does your brand appear? Being named first or recommended at the top carries more weight than a passing reference at the end.

**3\. Frequency**: How often does your brand come up across all the [prompts](https://trackbase.ai/features/prompt-tracking) and platforms tracked? One mention per week looks very different from twenty.

-   **4\. Context and** [**sentiment**](https://trackbase.ai/blog/being-mentioned-by-ai-isnt-enough-why-sentiment-decides-who-wins): Are you described positive, neutral or negative? A positive mention as “the leading platform for X” is worth far more than a neutral name-drop or, worse, a critical reference.

-   **5\. Comparative performance**: How do you rank versus [competitors](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-do-i-compare-ai-visibility-against-competitors) in the same answers? Visibility only matters relative to who else is in the room.

## Why mention count alone falls short

A simple count of mentions misses the difference between being recommended first and being mentioned as an afterthought. It also misses sentiment: a brand mentioned 50 times negatively is worse off than a brand mentioned 10 times glowingly.

By blending all 5 factors, the Visibility Score reflects the actual impact of your AI presence, not just the volume.

## How the daily refresh works

Every 24 hours, Trackbase runs a defined set of prompts across the [11 AI models](https://trackbase.ai/blog/the-11-ai-platforms-every-brand-should-be-tracking-in-2026). Prompts simulate real user queries in your category. The system parses every response, scores it on the 5 factors, and aggregates the result into your Visibility Score.

Because it runs daily, you can:

-

-   See the impact of content updates within days, not months

-

-   Catch sudden drops before they cost you customers

-

-   A/B test changes by tracking score movement over time

## What a healthy score looks like

There’s no universal target, context matters. A category-leading B2B SaaS in a niche market may sit at 70+. A new entrant in a crowded space may start in the 20s. What matters is the trend line and your [share of voice](https://trackbase.ai/blog/how-to-measure-ai-share-of-voice) versus direct competitors.
